A dynamic island for Windows.

WIland sits at the top of your screen and opens into what's going on: the song that's playing, your timers, files you've set aside and everything you copy.

What it is: a small island at the top of your screen for your music, timers, files and clipboard. It opens when you need it and stays out of the way when you don't.

What you need

Most PCs from the last few years.

The glass island is the most demanding part. On an older or slower PC, switch it to Black in the WIland app's Appearance page.

  • WindowsWindows 10 (version 2004 or later) or Windows 11, 64-bit. Windows 11 works best.
  • ProcessorA 64-bit Intel or AMD processor with 4 cores or more; 6 to 8 is best. ARM laptops, like Snapdragon ones, aren't supported yet.
  • Memory8 GB. 16 GB keeps the glass smooth next to your other apps.
  • GraphicsAny graphics from the last few years, built in or separate.
  • SpaceAbout 400 MB.
  • InternetFor updates, which WIland fetches by itself.

Install

Three steps, about a minute.

WIland isn't signed with a certificate yet, so Windows doesn't recognise it and warns you twice. Both warnings are normal for a test version.

  1. 01

    Keep the download

    If your browser says the file isn't commonly downloaded, choose Keep. In Edge, open the ⋯ menu next to the file, choose Keep, then Show more and Keep anyway.

    !WIland-Setup.exe isn't commonly downloaded. Make sure you trust it before you open it.
    DeleteKeep
  2. 02

    Run it anyway

    Open the file. When the blue Windows protected your PC box appears, click More info, then Run anyway.

    Windows protected your PC
    Microsoft Defender SmartScreen prevented an unrecognised app from starting.
    More info
    Run anywayDon't run
  3. 03

    Install and look up

    Go through the installer (the usual folder is fine) and click Finish. WIland starts, and the island sits at the top centre of your screen.

Everything at the top of the screen.

Hover to peek, click to open, and the island grows into the page you need.

Media

What's playing in Spotify, your browser or any player. Pause, skip and seek without switching apps.

Timers

Drag to set a length, tag it Study, Focus, Break or Work, and watch it count down in the pill.

Today

The date, your week and what's on, one step up from wherever you are.

Controls

Brightness and volume sliders, Bluetooth and Wi-Fi, and where your sound plays.

Shelf

Drop files on the island to set them aside, then drag them out where you need them.

Clipboard

Everything you copy is kept, so you can paste something from earlier.

You only download once.

WIland checks for new versions by itself, downloads them quietly and installs them the next time you restart it. You'll always be testing the latest build.

Getting around

Hover, click, drag.

  • PeekMove the pointer to the top centre of the screen.
  • OpenClick the island.
  • Switch pagesRight-click the left or right side of the open island, or swipe sideways on a touchpad.
  • Keep a fileDrag it onto the island.
  • Find an appPress Alt Shift Space for the launcher.
  • SettingsClick the WIland icon in the taskbar's tray.

WIConnect

Your PC, from your phone.

WIConnect is WIland's companion app for Android. It puts shortcuts for the app you're in, a pointer and keyboard, your PC's screens, timers, music and the Shelf on your phone.

Download for Android

Android 10 or newer · Free

  • PadShortcuts for the app in front on your PC, like After Effects, Premiere Pro and OBS Studio. Put buttons anywhere and size them.
  • Pointer and keyboardTurn your phone to point, or use it as a trackpad. Tap a text box on the PC to type into it.
  • Live screensEach of your PC's screens as live video, with the pointer moving on it. Touch the picture to work the mouse right there.
  • Apps and windowsOpen any app with one tap, bring a window forward, and save setups to put them back later.
  • Timers and musicTimers follow you between PC and phone and ring wherever you are. What's playing on the PC, with controls, on your lock screen too.
  • Files and clipboardCopy on one, paste on the other. Send files and folders to the Shelf, and save them from it.
  • PowerLock, sleep, restart or shut down the PC.

Set it up

Your phone and PC need to be on the same Wi-Fi.

  1. 01

    Install it on your phone

    Download the .apk on your phone and open it. Android asks once to allow installing apps from your browser; allow it, then install.

  2. 02

    Pair it with WIland

    In WIland on your PC, open Settings → WIConnect and show the pairing code. Scan it with WIConnect.

  3. 03

    Keep both up to date

    When there's a new version, WIConnect's home says WIConnect x.y.z is out. Tap it to update. New versions need a recent WIland, which updates itself.

Found a problem?

Right-click the WIland icon in the taskbar's tray and choose Report a bug. Say what went wrong; WIland adds a screenshot and sends it straight to the developer.
