What it does

Everything at the top of the screen.

Media

See what's playing in Spotify, your browser or any player, and pause, skip or seek.

Timers

Start a timer or a focus session and watch it count down without opening anything.

Battery and devices

Battery, Bluetooth and Wi-Fi at a glance, with volume and brightness sliders.

Shelf

Drag files onto the island to set them aside, then drag them out where you need them.

Clipboard

Everything you copy is kept, so you can paste something from earlier.

Launcher

Press Alt+Shift+Space to find and open apps and files.

Getting around

Hover, click, drag.

  • PeekMove the pointer to the top centre of the screen.
  • OpenClick the island.
  • Switch pagesRight-click the left or right side of the open island, or swipe sideways on a touchpad.
  • Keep a fileDrag it onto the island.
  • SettingsClick the WIland icon in the taskbar's tray.

You only download once.

WIland checks for new versions by itself, downloads them quietly and installs them when you restart it.
